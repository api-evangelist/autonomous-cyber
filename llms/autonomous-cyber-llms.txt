# Autonomous Cyber

> Autonomous Cyber is the maker of FUZZ-E, an AI platform for offensive cybersecurity ("The AI Platform for Hacking"). It supports commercial and government red teams with deep reconnaissance, adaptive custom tooling against AI-enabled defenses, and integrated intelligence workflows. The company states it works strictly in the American national interest. No public developer API is currently documented.
